01 What it is

It's an NFT. It's an agent. It has a wallet.

Also: not financial advice. Two meanings, three letters. The second one is the product.

.1

It's an NFT

Every agent is a Metaplex Core asset on Solana, owned by your wallet. Its look and its rules are written on the NFT itself.

.2

It's an agent

At mint it's registered on Metaplex's Agent Registry, with a public registration file: what it is, where to talk to it, and its robots.txt.

.3

It has its own wallet

Each agent gets a wallet derived from the NFT, with no private key anywhere. Money goes in freely; it only comes out when the NFT's owner signs.

.4

Sell the NFT, the agent goes with it

Transfer or sell the NFT and the new owner gets the agent, its wallet, its balance and its history. Nothing to migrate.

    06 Under the hood
    
      
    What's real, and where to check it.
    
      
    
        
    The NFT
    A Metaplex Core asset (program CoREENxT6tW1HoK8ypY1SxRMZTcVPm7R94rH4PZNhX7d). Traits and robots.txt are stored in its Attributes plugin, and the owner holds the authority to change them, so the rules follow the NFT.
    
        
    The registry
    Registered with Metaplex's Agent Registry (program 1DREGFgysWYxLnRnKQnwrxnJQeSMk2HmGaC6whw2B2p) pointing at a public registration file with its web page, chat endpoint and robots.txt.
    
        
    The wallet
    The Core asset signer: an address derived from the NFT with no private key. Money leaves only through Core's Execute instruction, which only the NFT's owner can sign.
    
        
    Trades
    Jupiter routes the swap; the swap runs inside Execute, so it happens from the agent's own wallet. The site refuses any trade outside robots.txt before your wallet is asked.
    
        
    Dry runs
    Every mint and action is simulated against Solana mainnet on the server first. If the simulation fails, nothing reaches your wallet and you see why.
    
        
    What it can't do
    It can't trade while you sleep: every trade needs the owner's signature. It can't hold your keys. The chat reads its real wallet and rules, but it doesn't make decisions for you.
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    07 FAQ
    
      
    Questions.
    
      
    What does minting cost?
    No .nfa fee. You pay Solana's rent for the NFT and its registry entry (about 0.005 SOL, refundable only by burning) and the network fee. The mint panel shows the exact number from a dry run before you sign.
    
      
    Can the agent spend my money?
    No. The agent's wallet is separate from yours, and nothing leaves it unless the NFT's owner signs. You decide what goes in.
    
      
    What happens when I sell it?
    The NFT moves to the buyer, and with it the agent's wallet, balance, rules and history. From then on only the new owner can move money out. Core assets trade on marketplaces that support Metaplex Core.
    
      
    Does it trade on its own?
    Not yet. It chats, writes status notes from its real wallet, and suggests at most one trade that fits its rules. You press the button and sign. robots.txt caps what it may ever be asked to do.
    
      
    Is .nfa part of Metaplex?
    No. .nfa is independent. It uses Metaplex's open programs (Core and the Agent Registry) and Jupiter's public API.
    
      
    Is there a token?
    $NFA isn't live yet. When it is, its address will appear in the black bar at the top of this page. Anything posted before that isn't us.
    
      
    Is this financial advice?
    It's literally in the name: not financial advice. Crypto can go to zero. Only put in what you can lose.
