    FAQ
    Questions.
    
      
    Is mint live?
    Not yet. When it opens, the mint button on this page turns on. Until then you can build your agent; the draft stays saved in this browser.
    
      
    What does minting cost?
    No nfa fee. You pay Solana's rent for the NFT and its registry entry (about 0.006 SOL) and the network fee. The mint tab shows the exact number from a dry run before you sign.
    
      
    Can the agent spend my money?
    No. Its wallet is separate from yours, and nothing leaves it unless the NFT's owner signs. You decide what goes in.
    
      
    What happens when I sell it?
    The NFT moves to the buyer, and with it the agent's wallet, balance, rules and history. From then on only the new owner can move money out.
    
      
    Does it trade on its own?
    Not yet. It chats, writes status notes from its real wallet, and suggests at most one trade that fits its rules. You press the button and sign.
    
      
    How do I connect on my phone?
    Tap Connect wallet, then Open in Phantom or Open in Solflare. The site opens inside the wallet's own browser, where connecting works.
    
      
    Where do I see my agents?
    In your inventory. Connect Phantom or Solflare (top right) and it reads what your wallet really holds.
    
      
    Is nfa part of Metaplex?
    No. nfa is independent. It uses Metaplex's open programs (Core and the Agent Registry) and Jupiter's public API.
